WHAT DO THE TOOLS AND WEAPONS OF MINECRAFT DO?

Tools and weapons in a Minecraft world help the player a lot in playing the game. They make progressing in a world much easier and faster once you get to higher tiers of tools.

TIERS OF MINECRAFT TOOLS

There are tiers of Minecraft tools based on what material or ore is used to make them. For example, wood is the lowest since it’s the easiest to craft, while netherite tools are the highest since they are the hardest to obtain.

WOOD TOOLS

Wood tools are among the first items you get when starting a Minecraft world. They are easy to make from wood and sticks and can even be used as fuel in furnaces or smokers.

STONE TOOLS

After crafting wood tools, players usually mine stone next. Stone tools allow faster mining and are essential for progression since they help obtain key ores like iron.

IRON TOOLS

Iron tools mark the start of the mid-game. They’re more durable and can mine diamonds and other advanced ores, allowing faster progress.

GOLD TOOLS

Gold tools have very fast mining speeds but low durability. However, they’re useful in the Nether, as Piglins won’t attack players wearing at least one piece of gold armor or using gold tools.

DIAMOND TOOLS

Diamond tools are powerful, durable, and a major milestone in the game. They can handle nearly any task efficiently and are required to progress to netherite tools.

NETHERITE TOOLS

Netherite tools are the best in the game—fast, durable, and lava-resistant. Obtaining them requires ancient debris from the Nether, making them a true end-game goal.

To summarize, the table below describes each tier of tools. The numbers from 1–5 show how good they are in different aspects.

Wood Stone Iron Gold Diamond Netherite
Mining Speed 1 2 3 4 5 5
Durability 1 2 2 1 5 5
Easy to Get 5 5 3.5 3 2 1
Usefulness 5 5 5 3 5 5
Beginner Friendly 5 4 3 3 2 1
